# بوصلة الحياة الرقمية الكريمة · Dignified Digital Life Compass

A bilingual (Arabic and English) self check prepared by Knowledge Group for the Dignified Digital Life initiative. In about five minutes a person answers 27 short questions and gets a personal report: their balance across family, health, community and digital life; a daily quality of life index; their operating drain; the hours technology gives back to them; and one decision for the week to simplify, automate or stop a recurring task.

**Live page:** https://assadi50.github.io/dignified-digital-life/

**Privacy.** Answers stay in the visitor's browser. The page asks for no name or email. Field trial participants can choose to send an anonymous result code to the trial team through Microsoft Forms.

**Field trial switch.** `config.json` holds the KGC Microsoft Forms pre-filled link, with `DDLCODE` as the placeholder answer. While it is empty, the trial button stays hidden.

This is a self reflection tool, not a health or psychological diagnosis. Weights and thresholds are initial and will be calibrated after the field trial.
